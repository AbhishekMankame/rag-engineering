## What is Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a way to make AI answers more reliable by combinging searching for relevant information and then generating a response. Instead of guessing based only on old training data, it first finds useful data from external sources (like documents or databases) and then use it to give a better answer.
- Fetches up-to-date data and reduces incorrect or made-up answers
- Works well with specialized data like medical or legal content
- No need to retrain the model every time new data come incorrect
- Can use user-specific data to give more relevant responses

For example, a platform like GeeksForGeeks has its own large collection of coding articles and tutorials. If a user asks a question, instead of giving a general answer, a RAG-based systekm can:
- Search relevant articles from their dataset
- Pick the most useful content
- Generate an answer based on that specific information

This way, the response is more accurate, aligned with the platform's content and actually helpful for the user.
